02

LineTracker

Real-time stock price & sports-odds alerts, delivered by email.
Web App

Set a target for a stock price or a sports betting line and get an email the moment it hits.

Demo

Highlights

  • Real Google OAuth authentication, verified securely on the backend, so every alert is locked to its owner and can’t be viewed/deleted by anyone else.
  • Background scheduler (APScheduler) polling live odds and stock data, with a Flask in-memory cache to stay under API rate limits.
  • Smart duplicate-game detection that catches games the odds provider accidentally lists twice, matching them up by team name and game time.
  • Email notifications through Brevo, backed by PostgreSQL for persistent storage on Render.
